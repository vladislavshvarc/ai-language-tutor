## Goal
Pronounce the four nasal vowels without saying the n, and know which final letters are silent.

## Explanation
- A vowel + **n/m** at the end of a syllable becomes nasal; the n/m itself is **not pronounced**.
  - **an, am, en, em** [ɑ̃]: enfant, temps, chambre
  - **in, im, ain, ein, un** [ɛ̃]: vin, pain, plein, un
  - **on, om** [ɔ̃]: bon, nom, maison
- Not nasal when n/m is followed by a vowel or doubled: bon [bɔ̃] / bonne [bɔn], un [ɛ̃] / une [yn], plein / pleine.
- Final consonants are usually silent: petit, grand, vous, est, trop. Often pronounced at the end: **c, r, f, l** ("CaReFuL"): sac, bonjour, neuf, sel. But verbs in **-er** have silent r: parler [paʁle].
- **-ent** at the end of a verb (ils parlent) is silent: ils parlent sounds like il parle. (Not in nouns/adverbs: le moment, souvent [ɑ̃].)
- **-s** of the plural is silent: les livres = [le livʁ].
- **h** is never pronounced. h muet (most words) allows elision/liaison: l'hôtel, les‿hôtels; h aspiré (marked * in dictionaries) blocks them: le héros, les | haricots.

## Forms
| Spelling | Sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| an, am, en, em | [ɑ̃] | enfant, temps, chambre |
| in, im, ain, ein, un | [ɛ̃] | vin, pain, plein, un |
| on, om | [ɔ̃] | bon, nom, maison |
| -ent (verb) | silent | ils parlent, elles mangent |
| final c r f l | pronounced | sac, bonjour, neuf, sel |
| other final consonants | silent | petit, grand, vous |

## Examples
- Un bon vin blanc. - A good white wine.
- Mon enfant a cinq ans. - My child is five.
- Ils parlent et ils mangent. - They talk and they eat.
- Le pain est sur la table. - The bread is on the table.
- C'est une bonne idée. - It's a good idea.

## Traps
- Pronouncing the n: «бон», «вин» → [bɔ̃], [vɛ̃].
- Reading -ent in verbs: «ils parl-ent».
- Reading final t/s/d: «пети», «гран» are right; «петит» is wrong.
- Russian speakers often nasalize bonne / une by analogy - with a vowel after the n the vowel is not nasal.

## Mistake patterns
pronounced n in nasal vowels; -ent read aloud; final -t/-s/-d read; une read as nasal; l'héros.

## Quick check
1. (audio: vent) vin / vent / vont → vent
2. Is -ent in "elles chantent" pronounced? → no
3. Which is nasal: bon / bonne → bon
