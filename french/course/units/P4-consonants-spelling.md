## Goal
Read and spell the consonant combinations that differ from Russian/English: r, c/g before e/i, s between vowels, ch, gn, ill, qu, th, and double letters.

## Explanation
- **r** is uvular (back of the throat, like a soft Russian «г»/«х» voiced), not rolled.
- **c**: [k] before a, o, u (café), [s] before e, i, y (ceci). **ç** keeps [s] before a/o/u (ça).
- **g**: [g] before a, o, u (gare), [ʒ] before e, i, y (rouge, gîte). **ge** keeps [ʒ] before a/o (mangeons); **gu** keeps [g] before e/i (guitare).
- **s** between two vowels = [z] (maison, rose); **ss** = [s] (poisson). poison ≠ poisson.
- **ch** = [ʃ] «ш» (chat, chercher). **gn** = [ɲ] «нь» (montagne, champagne). **ill** after a vowel = [j] (travail, fille [fij]) - exceptions: ville, mille, tranquille [il].
- **qu** = [k] (qui, quand). **th** = [t] (thé). **ph** = [f] (pharmacie).
- **j** = [ʒ] «ж» (je, jour).
- Double consonants sound single: belle [bɛl], appeler [aple].

## Forms
| Spelling | Sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| r | [ʁ] | rue, Paris, bonjour |
| c + e/i, ç | [s] | ceci, ça |
| g + e/i, j | [ʒ] | rouge, je |
| vowel + s + vowel | [z] | maison, rose |
| ss | [s] | poisson |
| ch | [ʃ] | chat |
| gn | [ɲ] | montagne |
| (vowel) ill | [j] | travail, fille |
| qu, th, ph | [k], [t], [f] | qui, thé, pharmacie |

## Examples
- La maison est rose. - The house is pink.
- Je mange du poisson. - I eat fish.
- Le chat est à la montagne. - The cat is in the mountains.
- La fille travaille à la pharmacie. - The girl works at the pharmacy.
- Nous mangeons à midi. - We eat at noon.

## Traps
- Rolled Russian «р» → uvular [ʁ].
- maison read with [s] → [mɛzɔ̃]; poisson vs poison.
- fille read [fil] → [fij]; but ville [vil].
- nous mangons (spelling) → nous mangeons; nous commencons → nous commençons.

## Mistake patterns
nous mangons, nous commencons, maison with [s], fille [fil], rolled r, the read as English th.

## Quick check
1. nous + voyager → nous voyageons
2. poisson or poison for "fish" → poisson
3. ch in "chercher" = ? → [ʃ]
