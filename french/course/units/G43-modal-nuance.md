## Goal
Express obligation, probability, possibility and advice precisely by choosing the right modal and tense.

## Explanation
- **devoir**:
  - present = must / have to (Je dois partir) or probability (Il doit être fatigué = he must be tired);
  - passé composé = had to / must have (J'ai dû attendre; Il a dû oublier = he must have forgotten);
  - imparfait = was supposed to (Je devais partir, mais…);
  - conditional = should (Tu devrais); past conditional = should have (Tu aurais dû).
- **pouvoir**: present = can/may; passé composé = managed to / might have (Il a pu oublier); conditional = could (Tu pourrais); past conditional = could have (Tu aurais pu).
- **falloir** (impersonal): il faut + infinitive / que + subjunctive; il fallait = it was necessary; il a fallu = we had to (one event); il faudrait = it would be necessary; il aurait fallu = we should have.
- **avoir à** + inf. = to have to (lighter): J'ai à faire. **être censé** + inf. = to be supposed to.
- Negative nuance: tu ne dois pas = you must not; tu n'es pas obligé de / il n'est pas nécessaire de = you don't have to.

## Forms
| meaning | form |
|---|---|
| must (now) | je dois / il faut |
| had to (once) | j'ai dû / il a fallu |
| was supposed to | je devais / j'étais censé |
| should | je devrais / il faudrait |
| should have | j'aurais dû / il aurait fallu |
| could have | j'aurais pu |
| must have (probability) | il a dû + inf. |

## Examples
- Il doit être malade, il n'est pas venu. - He must be ill, he didn't come.
- J'ai dû prendre un taxi. - I had to take a taxi.
- Nous devions partir à 8 h, mais le train avait du retard. - We were supposed to leave at 8, but the train was late.
- Il aurait fallu réserver. - We should have booked.
- Tu n'es pas obligé de venir. - You don't have to come.

## Traps
- «Tu ne dois pas venir» = you must not come (≠ you don't have to).
- «J'ai dû partir» vs «J'aurais dû partir» (had to vs should have).
- «Il faut que tu viens» → viennes (subjunctive).

## Mistake patterns
tu ne dois pas (for "don't have to"), j'ai dû for "should have", il faut que + indicative, je devrais (for "I had to").

## Quick check
1. «Надо было забронировать.» → Il aurait fallu réserver.
2. «Он, наверное, ушёл.» → Il a dû partir.
3. «Тебе стоит отдохнуть.» → Tu devrais te reposer.
