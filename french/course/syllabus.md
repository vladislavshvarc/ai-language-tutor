# French grammar course - syllabus

Teaching order. Pronunciation units come first: spelling-to-sound is what makes accents, silent letters and liaison make sense. A topic may pull a later unit forward when it needs it (e.g. a "last weekend" topic needs G15); then the course continues in order.
