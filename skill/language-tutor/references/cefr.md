# CEFR levels, placement test, topics, level-up

## Levels in one line each (what the learner can do)

| Level | Can do | Reading length | Writing task length |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | nothing yet / a few words | 3-5 very short sentences | 2-3 sentences with a model given |
| A1 | introduce self, simple present, everyday nouns, basic questions | 60-100 words | 3-5 sentences (30-50 words) |
| A2 | past events, routines, shopping, directions, simple connectors | 100-150 words | 50-80 words |
| B1 | narrate, give opinions with reasons, handle travel situations | 150-250 words | 100-150 words |
| B2 | argue, hypotheticals, most tenses/moods, abstract topics | 250-400 words | 150-250 words |
| C1 | nuance, idiom, register, complex texts | 400-600 words | 250+ words |

## Placement test (new language, not a self-declared total beginner)

10-15 questions of rising difficulty, delivered in 2-3 batches (use tappable options for multiple choice when available). Stop early if the learner clearly can't answer the current tier (e.g. misses most A2 items → don't show B1/B2).

Mix, roughly:
- **A1 (4 items)**: basic vocabulary (colours, family, food) by multiple choice; article/gender; present tense of être/ser/sein-type verb.
- **A2 (4 items)**: past tense form; a preposition/contraction; question formation; a short 3-4 sentence reading with 1 question.
- **B1 (3-4 items)**: choose the right past tense in context; relative pronoun; a 5-6 sentence reading with 2 questions (one inference).
- **B2 (2-3 items)**: subjunctive/conditional in context; a connector (although/however); an idiomatic phrase.

Scoring → level (adjust using judgement on *which* items were right):
- 0-3 correct, mostly wrong at A1 → **A0**
- A1 tier mostly right, A2 mostly wrong → **A1**
- A2 tier mostly right, B1 mostly wrong → **A2**
- B1 mostly right → **B1**; B2 mostly right → **B2** (suggest a C1 check later if everything was right)

Tell the learner the level, one sentence on why ("you know articles and present tense well; past tense is the next step"), and that it can change as they go. Save score and level to progress.md.

## Topic bank (don't repeat topics in the history; related-but-new is fine)

- **A0-A1**: greetings & introductions, family, numbers & prices, at the café, at the bakery, colours & clothes, my home, the car, days & time, weather, food shopping, my city, public transport, body & feeling ill, hobbies
- **A2**: missed train, booking a hotel, asking directions, at the doctor, a weekend trip, cooking a recipe, a birthday party, renting a flat, at the post office, my workday, lost luggage, the gym
- **B1**: job interview, complaining about a product, planning a holiday, news story (adapted), environment in my city, a film review, a problem with a neighbour, online shopping returns, a festival or tradition
- **B2+**: work-life balance debate, a real news article, technology & privacy, a podcast excerpt, negotiation at work, a short story extract, cultural differences, economy basics

Use the learner's interests from progress.md "Preferences" when choosing among these (e.g. someone who works with deliveries may enjoy "a delayed parcel").

## Level-up / level-down

- **Suggest level-up** when the last 3 topic exams at the current level are all ≥ 90%, or 5 of the last 6 are ≥ 85%. Offer a short level-up check (6-8 items from the next tier). Pass ≥ 75% → update Level + Level history in progress.md.
- **Suggest easing** when 2 exams in a row fail (< 80%): offer an extra review session or a simpler topic before moving on; don't lower the level without the learner agreeing.
